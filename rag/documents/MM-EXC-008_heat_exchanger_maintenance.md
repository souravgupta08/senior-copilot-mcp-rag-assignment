# Discharge Cooler Maintenance Manual (MM-EXC-008)

## Fouling Detection

Fouling is indicated when the outlet temperature rises above 55 degC at normal load, or when the temperature approach increases by more than 5 degC compared to the clean baseline.
Fouling of E-501 directly increases discharge temperature and pressure of Gas Compressor K-501 and K-502.

## Cleaning

Clean the tube side with high-pressure water jetting during a planned stop.
Check cooling water treatment records for scaling.

## Cooling Water Checks

Check cooling water supply temperature and flow. Low flow is often caused by a partly closed inlet valve or a blocked strainer.
