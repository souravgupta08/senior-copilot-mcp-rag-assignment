# Gas Compressor Troubleshooting Guide (TG-CMP-003)

## Repeated High Discharge Pressure Alarms

Compressor discharge pressure alarms that repeat several times per week are most often caused by:

1. Fouling of the discharge cooler (E-501). A fouled cooler raises gas temperature and discharge pressure. Look for "High Outlet Temperature" alarms on the cooler a few minutes before the compressor alarm.
2. Low cooling water flow or high cooling water supply temperature.
3. A downstream block valve or pressure control valve that is partly closed.
4. Anti-surge / recycle valve controller in manual mode.

Recommended actions: check cooler outlet temperature and cooling water flow, confirm the recycle valve is in automatic, and plan a cooler cleaning if the outlet temperature is above 55 degC.

## High Discharge Temperature

Check suction temperature, compression ratio and cooler performance.
A rising discharge temperature together with rising pressure points to a cooling problem.

## Surge

On a surge alarm open the anti-surge valve immediately and confirm the controller is in automatic. Do not operate the compressor left of the surge control line.

## Low Lube Oil Pressure

Start the standby lube oil pump. Check filter differential pressure and oil level.
Low lube oil pressure on Lube Oil Pump LOP-501 can trip Compressor Motor M-501.
