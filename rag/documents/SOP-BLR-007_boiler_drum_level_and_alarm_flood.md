# Boiler Drum Level and Alarm Flood Handling (SOP-BLR-007)

## Low Drum Level

Check feedwater flow and the boiler feed pumps. Confirm the drum level on two independent level gauges.
If level keeps falling, reduce firing rate. The low-low drum level trip must never be bypassed.

## High Flue Gas Temperature

Check soot blower operation and economizer performance. Plan a soot blowing cycle.

## Alarm Flood Handling

An alarm flood is more than 10 alarms in 10 minutes for one operator.
During a flood, focus first on critical and safety alarms, then high alarms.
After the flood, review the sequence of events to find the first alarm, which is usually the root cause.
