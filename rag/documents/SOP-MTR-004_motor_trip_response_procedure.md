# Motor Trip Response Procedure (SOP-MTR-004)

## Immediate Response to a Motor Trip

1. Check the protection relay to see which protection operated (overcurrent, earth fault, thermal, or process interlock).
2. Check whether a process interlock caused the trip, for example low lube oil pressure.
3. Do not attempt a restart before the cause is known.

## Related Assets to Inspect After a Motor Trip

After a motor trip inspect:

- the driven equipment (pump or compressor) for mechanical seizure,
- the lube oil system (Lube Oil Pump LOP-501 for Compressor Motor M-501),
- the cooling fan and air path of the motor,
- the motor cable and terminal box.

## Restart Rules

Measure insulation resistance before restarting a motor that tripped on earth fault or thermal protection.
A maximum of two restart attempts is allowed within one hour to avoid winding overheating.

## High Winding Temperature

Check motor current against the nameplate and check the cooling fan.
Clean blocked cooling air paths. Reduce load if the temperature keeps rising.
