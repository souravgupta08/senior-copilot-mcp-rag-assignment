# Alarm Response Safety Instructions (SAF-005)

## General Rules

Critical alarms must be acknowledged within 5 minutes and high alarms within 10 minutes.
Never bypass or inhibit a safety trip without a written bypass permit approved by the plant manager.

## Field Checks

Wear hearing protection, safety glasses and gloves for field checks on rotating equipment.
Keep a safe distance from hot surfaces and high-pressure lines.

## Isolation (Lock Out / Tag Out)

Before any maintenance work, isolate electrical supply and process connections and apply lock out / tag out.
Verify zero energy before starting work.

## Escalation

Escalate to the shift supervisor when a critical alarm cannot be cleared within 10 minutes or when the same alarm repeats more than three times in one shift.
