# Control Valve Troubleshooting Guide (TG-VLV-006)

## Valve Position Deviation

A valve position deviation alarm means the valve feedback does not follow the controller demand.
Likely causes: low instrument air pressure, positioner out of calibration, sticky valve stem (packing too tight) or a failing actuator diaphragm.

Actions: compare demand and feedback trends, check instrument air pressure at the positioner, and stroke-test the valve during the next opportunity.

## Effect on Pumps

A feedwater control valve that closes unexpectedly will raise the discharge pressure of the boiler feed pumps upstream.
If valve deviation alarms are followed within minutes by pump high discharge pressure alarms, treat the valve as the root cause.

## Valve Stuck

Do not hammer or force a stuck valve. Raise an urgent maintenance request.
